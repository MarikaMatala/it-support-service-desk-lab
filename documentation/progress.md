### Status definitions

- **Not started** — No exercise has been documented in this area yet.
- **In progress** — I am currently working on an exercise in this area.
- **Exploring** — I have completed at least one introductory exercise and am learning the fundamentals.
- **Practising** — I have completed multiple exercises and am building consistency.
- **Developing independence** — I can investigate familiar scenarios with limited guidance.
- **Confident in the lab** — I can handle a range of lab scenarios independently and explain my decisions.

These statuses describe learning progress only. They are not certifications or claims of professional proficiency.
